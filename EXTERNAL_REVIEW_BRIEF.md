# External Consultation Brief

Please evaluate this repository as an independent scientific reviewer. Focus on whether the released derived data, diagnostic ledgers, figure-ready tables and plotting code support the following bounded claim:

> Publicly accessible hourly weather-information support can be screened for residual lower-support cases in high-need urban low-altitude service contexts, and the released event diagnostics provide bounded precipitation-linked relevance without validating operational flight risk or direct three-dimensional low-altitude wind-field structure.

## Questions for review

1. Does the diagnostic design distinguish a residual support deficit from simpler explanations such as population, airport proximity, official station density, climate background, country dominance or denominator uncertainty?
2. Do the event-validation tables support only the bounded precipitation-linked relevance claim, or do any manuscript-facing interpretations risk overclaiming operational low-altitude risk?
3. Are the figure-ready source tables and plotting code sufficient for checking the main visual evidence?
4. What would be the strongest objections to field fit, novelty, practical service relevance and claim-evidence alignment?
5. What minimal revision would make the work clearer while keeping the evidence ceiling unchanged?

## Boundaries

Do not treat this repository as evidence for hidden national observing capacity, causal station-capacity failure, route-level disruption, direct visibility or ceiling hazards, or operational flight decision support. Raw third-party records are not redistributed here.
